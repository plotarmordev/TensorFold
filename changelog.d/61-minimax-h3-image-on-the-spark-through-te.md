# MiniMax-H3-Image on a Spark, no Metal and no PyTorch

- `tf_h3_open` loads a dense-only H3 checkpoint, MiniMax-H3-Image's `transformer/` blocks, which have no `attn.to_gate_compress`: each block keeps q, k and v alone, its fused per-head q, k, v rows stacked as `h3_heads` reads them. A routed forward (`keep` set) on such a model is refused with `BadH3Ungated`, and setting its gate with `NoGate`.
- `tf-h3-image-prompt-host`, `tf-h3-image-cuda` and `tf-h3-image-decoder-host` make a picture on a Spark: the prompt and the decoder on the CPU through their host oracles, the transformer on the CUDA blocks with the rows in, AdaLN tables, final norm and the step heads of `pdd_fused_heads.safetensors` on the host. Each writes the matching Metal tool's file, so stages swap. `tf-h3-image-cuda` reads the sharded checkpoint in place.
- `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda/run-script.sh` runs the three through the Desk's GPU door at 2048² and 4096². The first CUDA pictures match the Mac's at 29.2 dB PSNR (2048²); stage times and the mmh3 comparison are in `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda.md`.
