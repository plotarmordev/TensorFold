# MiniMax-H3-Image: a re-pipelined fused attention, measured and reverted

- The transformer's dense attention already runs as one fused kernel — bf16 scores on `mma.sync m16n8k16`, an fp32 online softmax and the value product on-chip (≈ 80 TFLOPS by the tile geometry, far off the old ~5%-of-peak path). This round tried to speed it further.
- `h3_attention` was re-pipelined without changing its launch or arithmetic: the query tile moved from shared memory into registers (freeing 16 KB), the freed space double-buffered the value tile a tile ahead, and each mma's B fragment was fetched ahead of its use. The output stayed element-for-element identical and every gate held — selftests, and the decoder's same-latent bound (86.117% of bytes identical, 99.916% within 1, at most 5 apart, PSNR 56.62 dB, relative error 3.16e-3 under 3e-2).
- Measured on TOP (job `top-309ccf152319`, `bench-script.sh`, commit `368cee6b1b59`) over a cold plus three warm 2048² runs and reverted for being slower: a warm picture took **19.0 s** mean (19.0, 19.0, 19.0: transformer 13.0, attention **1.91 s**, 2.15 s a step), cold 161.0 s, against the `c30403d9` baseline's **18.9 s** (18.9, 18.9, 18.9: transformer 12.8, attention **1.80 s**), cold 157.8 s. The register cost of the resident query fragments outweighed the deeper prefetch — attention's bound is not its value load's latency. The kernel is unchanged on this branch; the measurement is the round's receipt.
