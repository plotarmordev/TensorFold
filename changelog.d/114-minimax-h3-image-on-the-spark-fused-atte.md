# MiniMax-H3-Image: a re-pipelined fused attention, measured and reverted

- The transformer's dense attention already runs as one fused kernel — bf16 scores on `mma.sync m16n8k16`, an fp32 online softmax and the value product on-chip, well off the old ~5%-of-peak path. This round tried to speed it further.
- `h3_attention` was re-pipelined without changing its launch or arithmetic: the query tile moved from shared memory into registers (freeing 16 KB), the freed space double-buffered the value tile a tile ahead, and each mma's B fragment was fetched ahead of its use. The output stayed element-for-element identical and every gate held — selftests, and the decoder's same-latent bound (PSNR 56.62 dB, relative error 3.16e-3 under 3.00e-2, 86.1% of bytes identical).
- Measured on TOP over a cold plus three warm 2048² runs and reverted for being slower: a warm picture took **19.0 s** (transformer 13.0 s, attention **1.91 s**, 2.16 s a step) against the `b4c5c42` baseline's 18.2 s / 12.1 s / **1.80 s** / 2.02 s. The register cost of the resident query fragments outweighed the deeper prefetch — attention's bound is not its value load's latency. The kernel is unchanged on this branch; the measurement is the round's receipt.
