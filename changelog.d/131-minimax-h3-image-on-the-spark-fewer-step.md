# MiniMax-H3-Image on the Spark: an opt-in half-canvas draft start

- `tf-h3-image-cuda` grew `--draft-steps N` (and `--renoise-sigma X`), mmh3's `--draft-steps` ported verbatim: the first N steps run on the half-size canvas, the clean draft is enlarged bilinearly (`picture.enlargeDraft`, mmh3's `resize_video` taps), noised to the next sigma (or the renoise), and the remaining steps finish at full size. Off-grid steps blend the distilled output heads their sigma span covers (`picture.headSpan`, mmh3's `step_heads`) through chained `h3_rows_out` launches with an accumulate-and-scale argument; an on-grid step keeps its own head with weight 1 and bit-identical math.
- Chosen over the PDMD 2-NFE LoRA after studying both (see `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda.md`): the LoRA path needs weights staged to TOP's pilot folder plus an adapter through the int8 quantized GEMM stack, neither reachable in this card's run; drafts need only host-side resize and reuse every existing kernel.
- Measured and documented in the recipe; the default stays six steps and the flag draws a different picture by construction (it fails the episode-1 picture gates against the Mac PNG), so it is opt-in pending the lead's call on the side-by-side.
