# MiniMax-H3-Image on the Spark: load the transformer's weights with threaded in-place reads

- `docs/mmh3-h3-image-study.md` lever 7: mmh3's DiT load is 3.2 s on eight reader threads; ours read the 36.7 GiB of shards single-threaded through the mapping's faults (~90 s cold). `tf-h3-image-cuda --load-threads N` cuts every tensor's staged image into ≤8 MiB read jobs (`cuda_load_threads.zig`) that a crew of N readers `pread` straight into the pinned stage — `checkpoint.Entry` grew the shard's descriptor and absolute file offset for it — while the two 64 MiB stages alternate between a crew fill and the previous window's `cuMemcpyHtoDAsync` ranges (one range a destination run, so the fused qkv stacking lands as whole head parts). 0 keeps today's in-place reads.
- The latent and PNG are byte-identical to the 0-thread run and the warm picture is unchanged (the steps never touch the load path); a missing or short shard fails the header/shape pass or the fill before any step runs — the no-GPU tests in `cuda_load_threads.zig` cover both, plus the fused stacking and the inline fill.
