# Drama 3 pruned NVFP4 checkpoints on main
- `docs/drama-3-nvfp4.md` covers serving both published pruned checkpoints (7,369 and 5,900 routed experts kept) from main on a DGX Spark and a Mac, with the current test commands and measured limits.
- `tools/zig/drama3_checkpoints_check.py` serves each given checkpoint and fails unless its startup line holds the expected experts and fixed-seed whole and streamed replies equal their repeats and their concurrent lanes; it records load time, peak memory, first audio, total time and RTF. On an M5 Ultra both pruned checkpoints pass, load in under 9 s and speak at an RTF of 0.73 and 0.74. On a DGX Spark FP8 and both pruned checkpoints pass: 39.6, 21.7 and 19.3 GiB device peak, first audio 6.07, 5.69 and 5.65 s, RTF 2.36, 2.16 and 2.23 with guidance.
- First-audio timing now uses the first available response bytes, with a delayed HTTP-stream regression test. The Mac receipt labels its older buffered-read latency as an upper bound and reports concurrent first audio at 6.07/6.22 s with RTF 1.90.
- `docs/receipts/drama3-pruned-main/prebuild-fatbins.sh` builds a commit's sm_121 kernel images on the Spark into the Drama pilot folder; `run-script.sh` checks them by sha256, builds with `-Dfatbins` and puts the CUDA mount's cuBLAS on `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`, so the GPU job image needs no C++ compiler.
