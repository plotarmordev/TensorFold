# MiniMax-H3-Image on the Spark: GPU decode

- Replace the decoder's cuBLASLt products with a shared-memory tiled FP32 kernel whose every output sums K in increasing order with individually rounded multiplies and adds. The cause of the 86.9-87.3% block identity was the cuBLASLt bf16 tensor-core products: swapping only those products for IEEE-rounded FP32 sums, with every other kernel unchanged, moved 6×10 block 1 from 86.952% to 97.912% against the float64 reference. That the tensor-core MMA's FP32 accumulation, not rounded at each add, is the mechanism on sm_121 is inferred, not profiled.
- Keep the 6×10 decoder selftest's 90% BF16-identity gate unchanged. The failing-before test is that selftest on the Spark: card 71's job `top-587035ea34ef` (`d3ca6199`, `h3-image-runs/71/d3ca6199`) failed block 1 at 86.952%; job `top-52e442a99176` (`6f041768`, `h3-image-runs/72/6f041768`) passed it at 97.912%, the larger block 1 at 93.123%, with the same sums this tiled kernel gives. The `h3d_linear` kernel contract checks those sums at the decoder's reduction depths and at ragged tile edges.
- Spark benchmark job `top-74801542a2fc` (`1eedb846`, `h3-image-runs/72/1eedb846`) passed the transformer, decoder, and prompt selftests. At 2048² the decoder took 5.6 s cold and in all three warm runs. Its 91.201% picture-byte line is the 20×28-cell synthetic selftest's two-block picture, not a 2048² decode; the 2048² runs record output and timing only.
- Add `tf-h3-image-decoder-cuda reference MODEL latent.f32 ROWS COLUMNS gpu.png host.png [SIZE]`: it decodes one latent on the GPU and on the host reference decoder, writes both PNGs and compares the pictures against the selftest's 3e-2 full-chain relative bound, reporting the bytes identical, within 1, the largest difference and PSNR. `bench-script.sh` runs it on the 2048² latent after both sizes, in `2048-reference.compare.log`. On that job's latent the M5 Ultra's host reference decode against its GPU PNG gives 86.180% of bytes identical, 99.923% within 1, at most 5 apart and 56.65 dB, within the 3e-2 bound; the Spark window's own run is pending. The selftest's 2-byte picture bound is measured after two blocks and does not cover a 24-block decode.
- The ordered FP32 sums cost speed: 5.23 s of GPU time at 2048² against card 71's 2.80 s cuBLASLt run, about 1.9× slower on the GB10. A faster ordered-sum kernel is follow-up work.
