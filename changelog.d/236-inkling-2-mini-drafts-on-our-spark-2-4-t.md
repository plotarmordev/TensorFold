# Inkling 2 Mini CUDA: the DSpark draft head
- Load the DSpark head from `mtp.safetensors` and run it on CUDA (`inkling2_dspark`): committed taps fused into per-layer context rings, the three-layer block on the target's bf16 lane GEMM, then seven Markov steps whose greedy picks feed the next step on the device, with no host round trip between drafts.
- Check every draft step's logits against a float64 host reference of the same head on seeded context rows (`tf-cuda-test inkling2-dspark`, `docs/recipes/inkling-2-mini-cuda/dspark.sh`): the RMS error, the worst single logit, and each greedy pick within that margin of the reference's best, with a planted context-blind reference that must go red.
- Put all ten of the head's launches under the CUDA kernel contract (`kernel-contract "inkling2 dspark"`) — the tap fuse, ring context, init norm, Q/K norm and rope, ring attention, residual norm, SwiGLU, kappa, Markov step and capped pick, each with a planted control — with nvcc 13.0 compile-usage records for SM 80, 86, 89, 120 and 121. Later compiler releases remain pending until measured.
