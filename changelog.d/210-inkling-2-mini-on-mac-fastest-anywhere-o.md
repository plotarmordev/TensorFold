# Inkling 2 Mini on Metal: the open M5 work merged onto main

- Takes sethforprivacy's Metal stack from ashhart/TensorFold: the #4689 head, which carries #4446, #4447 (DSpark attention scale 1/sqrt(128), the recurrent step reads kappa as FP32), #4448, #4523, #4608, #4618, #4631, #4644, #4645, #4649 and #4666 (tensor-unit experts and prompt projections, 1,024-row prompt chunks with direct key tiles, one-pass round logits, fused convolution steps); #4660 with #4465 (the DSpark hold in one command buffer on the tensor units, drafts priced by the engine); #4607 (a snapshot copies each key-value head's live rows); #4622 (the window fit prices a drafted token at a token's bytes); #4635 (grammar masks step by the padded vocabulary).
- Takes gilby's #4542 for its DSpark lane state in the prompt cache; its key-value copy is the same fix as #4607, whose layout is kept.
- Leaves out gilby's #4543, #4683 and #4685 and the EXL3 packs in #4471: each rewrites the same kernels, plans and contracts as the stack in parallel (87 and 16 conflicting files), and #4471 serves another checkpoint format, not the official one the bar is measured on.
- Measured on the M5 Ultra with the official checkpoint (`docs/receipts/inkling-2-mini-metal-210`, medians of three, control/candidate/control): 61-62 tok/s plain and 120.7 with DSpark at one stream on code, 220 and 279.6 total at four, 2,439 tok/s prefill at 45K, against a 1,072 GB/s ceiling of 193 / 576 tok/s at one stream and 412 / 1,091 at four (plain / DSpark); 0 failed requests, serving fresh and restored equal to one serial stream on the backend. DSpark ties #4618's 123 / 281 within 2%, not past it; a six-rep rerun is void (its plain controls disagree by 29%). The Metal greedy output differs at 0 of 32 tokens from the host float64 reference (the planted no-messages control differs), adapted from Jürgen Schmied's (@jschmied) `mini_ref.zig` in his closed draft #4441.
- The Mini CUDA forward refuses a prompt or draft id past the vocabulary before it reaches the embedding, and the router top-k gives a row with fewer non-NaN candidates than picks id -1 at weight 0 for the rest, which the expert kernels skip.
- The H3 CUDA limits job takes its images from `LIMITS_IMAGES`; `docs/recipes/inkling-2-mini-cuda/limits-job.sh` runs it for the Mini's three images, compile only, then the top-k contract on sm_121. Its nvcc record on all five SMs replaces the zero-register placeholders; `zig build test test-golden` is green.
