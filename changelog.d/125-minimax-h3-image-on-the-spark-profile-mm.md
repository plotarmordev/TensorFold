# MiniMax-H3-Image: mmh3's warm server profiled on TOP, the gap table measured

- `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda/mmh3-server-job.sh` runs mmh3's `server` on TOP's GB10 (`cuda,server` build): one cold 2048² picture, three warm repeats of the pilot prompt and one 1024² request, then a second server under `cupti-trace.c` (an LD_PRELOAD CUPTI shim, since nsys is absent from the job image) traces every kernel launch for three warm pictures.
- The CUPTI trace (11,693 launches) splits mmh3's 8.7 s transformer as `int8_gemm` 5.55 s (all linears, ~953 TFLOP, ~172 TOPS), dense BF16 attention 1.78 s (~82 TFLOPS), fused norm+quantize 1.34 s; its VAE decode is 0.62 s wall. `bench-trace-job.sh` ran our own `bench-script.sh` under the same preload in the same window (`top-7abf48024020`, warm 15.4 s): our s8 GEMMs 4.32 s at ~154 TOPS (667 TFLOP), nvjet BF16 3.42 s at ~84 TFLOPS (`out`/`fc2`, 286 TFLOP), attention 1.75 s (~83 TFLOPS, equal to mmh3's), and 4.20 s of separate dequantize/heads/norm/quantize/swiglu passes against mmh3's 1.34 s of fused ones — the 4.1 s transformer kernel gap is products 2.2 s + unfused passes 1.9 s.
