# MiniMax-H3-Image: mmh3's warm server profiled on TOP, the gap table measured

- `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda/mmh3-server-job.sh` runs mmh3's `server` on TOP's GB10 (`cuda,server` build): one cold 2048² picture, three warm repeats of the pilot prompt and one 1024² request, then a second server under `cupti-trace.c` (an LD_PRELOAD CUPTI shim, since nsys is absent from the job image) traces every kernel launch for three warm pictures.
- Measured on TOP (jobs `top-a49336186ba9`, `top-6d2b3918ed7c`): mmh3's warm 2048² picture is **9.33 s** mean (9.31, 9.33, 9.37), cold 17.2 s — its 9.5 s quote confirmed against our warm **15.5 s** at `40ab38a8` (prompt 1.1, transformer 13.0, decoder 1.3).
- The CUPTI trace (11,693 launches) splits mmh3's 8.7 s transformer as `int8_gemm` 5.55 s (all linears at ~167 TOPS), dense BF16 attention 1.78 s (~82 TFLOPS, equal to ours), fused norm+quantize 1.34 s; its VAE decode is 0.62 s wall. The 4.6 s transformer gap is the products' rate (~167 vs ~110 TOPS) plus `out`/`fc2` (262 TFLOP) mmh3 runs in int8 where ours stay BF16; the rest of the wall gap is kept prompt states (−1.1 s) and the decoder (−0.7 s).
- `docs/mmh3-h3-image-study.md` gains the measured stage table, the per-kernel achieved-rates table beside ours, and a re-ranked lever list; `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda.md` references the server job. No engine code or defaults changed.
