# MiniMax-H3-Image benchmark run on the Spark

- `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda/bench-script.sh` runs through the Desk's GPU door like `run-script.sh`, with the same prompt, seed 42 and steps at 2048² and 4096². It records each CUDA tool's selftest PASS or FAIL and renders anyway. Each size gets one cold picture and three warm repeats, with per-stage times. `nvidia-smi` samples give peak memory, average watts and energy per picture. `run-script.sh` keeps its selftest gate.
- `tools/zig/h3_image_bench.py` writes `SUMMARY.md`, headed by the selftests' verdict (a failing build's timings are marked as not the Spark's result), with every number, the commit and the settings, including after a failed stage. Its stages are prompt, load, the server's per-picture case setup, transformer and decoder. `test_h3_image_bench.py` covers its parsing without a GPU.
- The CUDA prompt kernels `h3p_norm`, `h3p_heads` and `h3p_attention` now sum in the host reference's order: squares in sequence, two-pass softmax. Their tree reductions and online softmax broke the vision rows' float32-noise gate (2.86e-2, and a mismatch already after layer 0) where the host's float32 run matched float64 exactly.
- Record the `d3ca6199` benchmark window on TOP's GB10. Transformer and prompt selftests pass. The decoder's 6 x 10 block-1 identity is 86.952% against its 90% bound: an open gate failure owned by card 72, so these timings are not for card 68 until it passes.
- `tf-h3-image-cuda MODEL --serve` keeps the transformer loaded and renders one `case prefix` line of stdin at a time; the benchmark's warm repeats run on it, so only the cold run pays the load.
- `tf-h3-image-cuda` drops its mapped transformer shards' pages once the weights are on the device. Holding them for `--serve`'s lifetime kept a second copy of the weights in the GB10's shared memory, which slowed the warm prompt runs (88-119 s against 60.6 s cold) and left the 4096² decoder out of memory at `7079fd3b`. At `8293c6d7` the 4096² decoder completes (11.1 s); the warm prompt is still slower than cold (80.7 s against 45.5 s at 2048², 90.2 s against 17.4 s at 4096²), recorded as open in the recipe.
- `kernel-contract-limits` no longer opens a driver. `tf-cuda-limits DIR [IMAGE...]` runs it without building tf-cuda-test's contracts, and `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-cuda/limits-script.sh` uses it to audit the H3 images' records and the controls against the five served SMs' fatbins on any host with nvcc, no GPU.
- `h3p_norm`, `h3p_heads` and `h3p_attention`'s CUDA usage records hold the usage `limits-script.sh` measured at `8293c6d7`; the old records were stale after the summation-order change.
