# MiniMax-H3-Image: weights read ahead

- `tf-h3-image-prompt`, `tf-h3-image` and `tf-h3-image-decoder` read the weight bytes they use into the page cache with 16 parallel 8 MiB reads before Metal touches their in-place mappings, instead of faulting them in one page at a time. A cold picture on an M5 Ultra drops from 229 s to 61 s at 2048x2048 and from 288 s to 123 s at 4096x4096; the case, latent and PNG are byte-identical to before.
- `docs/recipes/minimax-h3-image-metal.md` holds the native before-and-after times, byte-identity gates and ranked speed ideas. The earlier cross-stack ranking is withdrawn: diffusers' first-picture time included a custom weight conversion, ComfyUI's warm 2048 used seed 43, and matched-input latent/image gates were missing. The corrected diagnostic diffusers totals include PNG saving; no win over every other stack is established.
- Same-noise, seed-42 ComfyUI reruns at 2048 and 4096 execute all six steps without cached nodes. Both pass latent cosine 0.99 but fail the fixed RGB difference bound; numerical results are retained, not waived. `tools/zig/h3_image_compare.py` provides the reproducible comparison and rejection tests.
- The comparator rejects partial trailing F32 elements instead of silently ignoring them; boundary and supported Comfy latent-layout checks run with `python tools/zig/h3_image_compare_test.py`.
- Pinned diffusers weights now pass SHA-256 verification. The documented loader takes 129.383 s; matched-noise requests including PNG saving take 24.817/16.633 s at 2048 and 102.038/103.640 s at 4096. Both sizes pass the latent bound but fail the fixed pixel bound; no quality-qualified cross-stack win is claimed.
