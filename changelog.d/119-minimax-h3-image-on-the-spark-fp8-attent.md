# MiniMax-H3-Image: FP8 attention with the heads prologue folded in, measured and reverted

- `h3_attention_fp8` ran the dense image attention on `mma.sync m16n8k32`: it staged the block's raw QKV rows, ran `h3_heads`' q/k RMSNorm and split-half rotary in its prologue, and quantized on-chip so the `h3_heads` pass and its TQ/TK/TV tiles dropped out of the dense step. Two precision splits were measured on TOP with `fp8-attn-bench.sh` (flag-off then flag-on in one window), every selftest and the decoder's same-latent bound passing in both.
- `acda1e04` (job `top-b76b0efc4246`), per-tile-absmax e4m3 on every operand: a warm 2048² picture took **39.4 s** (attention 22.8 s against `h3_attention` + `h3_heads`' 2.8 s) with the latent cosine **0.8695** — four significand bits over a whole tile is too coarse for Q·K.
- `fb5090ae` (job `top-ea3a4d434c59`), q and k int8 at each row's absmax with e4m3 kept only on the weighed-score × value product (mmh3's split): warm **30.3 s** (attention 14.1 s), cosine **0.9882**, 24.5 dB, against the same job's flag-off **18.9 s**. Both gates still failed: 0.988 under the 0.99 cosine, 30.3 s over the 18.9 s.
- Reverted in full: the serialized stage→quantize→mma prologue costs ~11 s a picture against `h3_attention`'s pipelined tile loop — the GB10 pays more to convert and scale into FP8 than the narrower mma returns at this tile size — and the last 0.002 of cosine likely needs the weighed scores in int8 too rather than e4m3. The kernels are unchanged from main; the measurements are the round's receipt.
