# MiniMax-H3-Image: e4m3 attention with the heads prologue folded in, measured short of the gates

- `h3_attention_fp8` runs the dense image attention on `mma.sync m16n8k32` in e4m3 behind `H3_ATTN=fp8`: the kernel stages the block's raw QKV rows, runs `h3_heads`' q/k RMSNorm and split-half rotary in its prologue, and quantizes q, k, v and the weighed scores at their tile's absmax (the P codes carry v's absmax so the value product's multiplier is the constant 1/448). The `h3_heads` pass and its TQ/TK/TV tiles drop out of the dense step when the flag is on.
- Measured on TOP (job `top-b76b0efc4246`, `fp8-attn-bench.sh` running `bench-script.sh` flag-off then flag-on, commit `acda1e04`) and kept opt-in: every selftest passed and the decoder's same-latent bound held (85.643% of bytes identical, 99.897% within 1, at most 5 apart, PSNR 56.46 dB, relative 3.34e-3 under 3e-2), but the fp8 run's latent cosine against the bf16 run was **0.8695** (gate ≥ 0.99) — the picture is wrong, not just quantized — and attention took **22.8 s** a picture against `h3_attention` + `h3_heads`' 2.8 s (warm 2048² picture 39.4 s against the flag-off 19.2 s). The serialized stage-quantize-mma loop and the doubled norm arithmetic cost far more than the halved mma count saved.
- The kernel stays behind the flag for the follow-up that finds the wrong indexing; the speed target needs the prologue pipelined like `h3_attention`'s, not a correctness fix alone.
