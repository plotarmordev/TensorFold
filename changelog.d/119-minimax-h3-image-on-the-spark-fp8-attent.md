# MiniMax-H3-Image: int8-key attention with the heads prologue folded in, measured short of the gates

- `h3_attention_fp8` runs the dense image attention on `mma.sync m16n8k32` behind `H3_ATTN=fp8`: the kernel stages the block's raw QKV rows, runs `h3_heads`' q/k RMSNorm and split-half rotary in its prologue, and quantizes on-chip so the `h3_heads` pass and its TQ/TK/TV tiles drop out of the dense step. Q and K quantize to int8 at each row's absmax (the row scales fold into the score rescale); the weighed scores and V stay e4m3, mmh3's split, with the V absmax carried on the P codes so the value product's multiplier is the constant 1/448. Each key tile's stage load is issued during the previous tile's value product.
- Two TOP jobs on `fp8-attn-bench.sh` (flag-off then flag-on in one window): `acda1e04` at per-tile-absmax e4m3 for all operands measured a warm 2048² picture of 39.4 s with a latent cosine of 0.8695 — four significand bits is too coarse for Q·K. `fb5090ae` at the int8 split measured **30.3 s** warm (attention **14.1 s** a picture against `h3_attention` + `h3_heads`' 2.8 s) with the latent cosine **0.9882**, against the same job's flag-off **18.9 s**. Selftests and the decoder's same-latent bound passed in both; the gates (0.99 cosine, 18.9 s) did not.
- The kernel stays behind the flag: what is left is not tuning — the serialized stage→quantize→mma prologue costs ~11 s a picture against `h3_attention`'s pipelined tile loop, and the last 0.002 of cosine likely needs the weighed scores in int8 too. A follow-up that pipelines the prologue and finishes the precision split starts from this measured base.
