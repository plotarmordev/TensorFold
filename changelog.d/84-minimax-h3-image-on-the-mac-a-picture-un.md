# MiniMax-H3-Image: a warm picture's stages, the decoder in two passes, and H3's own products

- `tf-h3-image-server TEXT_ENCODER_DIR MODEL_DIR profile` prints each request's six steps by stage, each stage in its own command buffer (slower; diagnostic). `h3.Model.profile` and its stage names already existed; the server exposes the switch and `image.zig` re-exports `stages`.
- On an M5 Ultra (256 GiB, macOS 27.0), card 34's `prompt.txt`, seed 42, 2048x2048, one resident server at load 12: a warm request's stages were `mlp in` 3.39 s, `q k v gate` 2.48 s, `mlp out` 2.20 s, `attention` 1.54 s, `attention out` 0.89 s, `norms` 0.14 s, `heads` 0.10 s and `rows in`/`final` 0.03 s, 10.69 s of GPU across the six steps. The biggest block is the fc1 product (28,672 x 5,376).
- The four products' threadgroups now run H3's own `h3_gemm_32x64` kernel (`h3_gemm.metal`) instead of MLX's `gemm_nax`: 64 x 128 a threadgroup, each warp 32 x 64 with 128 threads, so every loaded fragment feeds two MMAs. Its K chain is MLX's, 32-deep steps in order, so every output's sums and the latent are unchanged; `test-h3-gemm` holds it against MLX's kernel bit for bit across a band edge, a whole band and a ragged tail.
- At the production shapes (4,120 rows) it reaches 116 TFLOP/s on fc1 (28,672 x 5,376), 116 on q/k/v (21,504 x 5,376) and 108 on the out product (5,376 x 7,168), where MLX's kernel gets 118, 118 and 113: the products are at the tensor units' rate, and a wider tile is not the lever. The one shape it wins is fc2 (5,376 x 14,336), 103 against 89 TFLOP/s, which is 0.3 s of the six steps.
- The decoder now decodes up to 64 tiles a pass instead of 16 (two passes at 2048x2048 instead of eight). The latent and PNG are byte-identical (sha1 `f3563c56…` / `904814d6…`).
- A warm 2048 picture took 12.12 and 12.13 s (prompt 0.22, 6 steps 10.49 and 10.52, decoder 1.38 and 1.40), against episode 1's 13.03 s and the 12.30 s of this card's first commit. The 11 s target is not met, and no single block is left to cut: a stage's seconds cover all 300 block calls of the six steps, so `mlp in` is 11.3 ms a block at 112 TFLOP/s and `attention` 5.1 ms a block at 96, the products' own rate. Six steps are 1,006 TFLOP of work in 10.7 s, about 94 TFLOP/s against the 118 the fc1 shape reaches on MLX's kernel and 116 on ours.
- int8 fc1 on the tensor units (215 TOP/s on the fc1 shape against 96 for bf16 in a probe) is still not landed. Both operands at a row's absolute max took a warm 2048 picture to 10.99 s, but its picture missed card 80's floor (cosine 0.994759, PSNR 28.342 dB, mean pixel diff 4.365 against 4.1, max 243); fc2 in int8 fails outright (cosine 0.943, mean 14.8) since SwiGLU's rows have outliers. fc1's weights grouped per 256 values of K hold the rule (cosine 0.995344, 28.240 dB, mean 4.011, max 245) but the per-group fp32 fold costs 0.10 s a step: 9.63 s of steps, 11.35 s a picture, past the 11 s target. fp16 operands run at bf16's rate. The recipe records the measurements and the host costs (private int8 storage and a residency-set join cut a step's encode from 0.42 to 0.04 s).
