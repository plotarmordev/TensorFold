# MiniMax-H3-Image on the Spark: fuse the transformer's elementwise passes like mmh3

- `docs/mmh3-h3-image-study.md` lever 2: mmh3's transformer runs one preprocessing launch a block — gated residual add, modulated RMSNorm, Hadamard rotate and quantize in one pass; our int8 path ran `h3_norm` + `h3_quantize` before the qkvc product, `h3_dequantize` + `h3_heads` after it, and `h3_swiglu_int8` after the fc1 product — ~3.2 s of elementwise launches a picture against mmh3's 1.34 s fused.
- `h3_norm_quant` folds the gated add, modulation and quantize into one launch (the normed row is never materialized — each lane recomputes its four normed values as `modulated` rounded to bf16, bit-for-bit what `h3_quantize` reads); `h3_heads_i8` reads the qkvc product's int32 sums and scales and runs `h3_heads`' q/k RMSNorm + split-half rotary on the dequantized values — once per row, not per tile pair (card 120's fold-into-attention measured 11x slower). `h3_swiglu_int8` stays its own launch over the fc1 product's int32 sums: mmh3 gets the swiglu inside its own int8 GEMM's epilogue, but ours is cuBLASLt, whose i8×i8→i32 descriptors take no epilogue (the epilogue attribute's set is none/bias and applies to fp outputs). The bf16 path and the clip forward are unchanged. The card's "silu(gate)*up in fc1's epilogue, replacing h3_swiglu_int8" clause asks for what only an own int8 GEMM could carry: it wants its text amended to keep `h3_swiglu_int8` a launch (the fusion it names is the one product in the path that has no epilogue slot).
- Both fusions keep the launch they fold's arithmetic: `h3_norm_quant` reuses `h3_norm`'s thread stride, `block_sum_serial` squares and `modulated` rounding, then `h3_quantize`'s absmax/rounding verbatim; `h3_heads_i8` rounds each dequantized value exactly as `h3_dequantize` does (`bf(acc*sx*sw)` on the product's f32 scale) before the identical head loop. Output is bit-identical to the unfused chain — verified by the new `fused_test` device regression in `tf-h3-image-cuda --self-test` and by sha256-identical latents in the same-job A/B.
- Measured on TOP (job `top-e611c4df9602`, commit `5d4bd40` — the card's files over main's cards 124, 125, 127 and 131 — a same-job A/B: `bench-script.sh` on this commit, then `bench-128-base.patch` reverse-applied and `bench-script.sh` again): a warm 2048² picture took **13.9 s** mean (13.9, 13.9, 14.0: transformer 12.5–12.6, decoder 1.3–1.4 s) against the same job's baseline at main **14.3 s** (14.3, 14.3, 14.3: transformer 12.9–12.9). Per stage: `q k v gate` 2.68→1.88–1.89 s (the quantize launch is gone), `heads` 0.96→1.40–1.41 s (the fused kernel reads int32 and dequantizes per lane), `mlp in` 3.39→3.32–3.34 s — the elementwise 3.20 s the study counted is 2.4 s of launches (the remaining 0.83 s is `h3_swiglu_int8`, which cuBLASLt's int8 product cannot carry in an epilogue). All eight latents are sha256-identical (`8d067d75…`) between head and baseline, every selftest passed in both phases, and the picture gates from the first A/B — latent cosine **0.9952**, **28.69 dB**, **4.01** mean diff — hold unchanged: the latent is byte-identical.
